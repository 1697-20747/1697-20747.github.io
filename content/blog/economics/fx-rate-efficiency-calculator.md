## Overview

Short post covering a basic method to compare FX spreads across supplier. Self explanatory for the most part. Uses Bid Ask spread difference to calculate a decay factor, which is expressed as how many turns of conversion until your cash is consumed by the spreads.

For example, USD converted into EUR, then back other way, continuously. Until you have no money left. The use case is really aimed at high street or airport FX converters, when you need to quickly choose from several at a point in time, with no clear way to compare.

I will probably build this into a webscraper site at some point.
