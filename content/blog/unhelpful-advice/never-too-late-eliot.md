> "It's never too late to be who you ought to have been." — commonly attributed to George Eliot.

Except she probably never wrote it. Quote Investigator traces this one to greeting cards and inspirational-poster circulation, not to Middlemarch or anything else in Eliot's actual output. Doesn't make it bad advice, though.
