> "In the British Army, there are no good battalions and no bad battalions, no good regiments and no bad regiments. There are only good and bad officers." - General Slim. Defeat into Victory is an account of the retaking of Burma by Allied forces during the Second World War by the British Field Marshal William Slim. 

This is the same as the Napoleon quote of "No poor soldiers, only poor officers", which is the same concept of only poor workers blame their tools. The wider extrapolation here is that if it goes wrong, you have to own it. It is, quite literally, a 'you' problem. Even if the staff are incompetent, it can only be so because you tolerated incompetence or you hired the incompetent.
