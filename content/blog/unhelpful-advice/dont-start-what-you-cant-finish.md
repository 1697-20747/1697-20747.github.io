> "The guerrilla wins if he does not lose. The conventional army loses if he does not win". Henry Kissinger, 1969 Foriegn Affairs. Beware the asymetry of non conventional conflicts. Don't start what you haven't the stomach to see through to the better end. War should be an all or nothing committment. Nothing is a good choice most of the time. But in between may lie intelligent battles to ensure the attrition of the capacity of your enemy.
