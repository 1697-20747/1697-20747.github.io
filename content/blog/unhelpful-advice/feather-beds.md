> "We may not look at our pleasure to go to heaven in feather beds; it is not the way, for our Lord himself went thither with great pain, and by many tribulations, which was the path wherein he walked thither." — Thomas More, recorded in William Roper's *The Life of Sir Thomas More* (1556). Often shortened to "We cannot go to heaven in featherbeds."
