> You cannot carry the stone alone.
>
> un named F1 source, reference by Andrew Benson.

Carry the stone alone. Ferrari F1 team culture context, open and trustful or fearful. BBC FP3 Jun 13 2026, Barcelona GP. Discussion was about the failure of Ferrari to deliver a world championship in Formula one since 2008, despite having the budget, team, world class drivers and the reputation and finances to do so. Why not? If you are overprotective or you manipulate the position or role, you end up creating a circumstance where you carrying the stone on your own. You can’t do that as the stone is too heavy. The stone here is the metaphor of the burden of the collective task. If the burden is substantial, only the team action in willing unison can carry the stone. The implication here is unstated culture. If there is no team, willing subordination to the greater aspiration, then you can't succeed. Why try to be innovate and exposure yourself to failure when you won't be supported? The discussion went back to the Schumacher years, when there was a tight-knit team that insulated the staff from management interference from above. Ferrari in the dominant years was led by Team Principal Jean Todt and Technical Director Ross Brawn, creating a highly successful, dominant core group within Maranello. The implication was to Aston Martin today. Andrew Benson FP3 discussion.
