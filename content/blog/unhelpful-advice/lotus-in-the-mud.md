> The lotus blossoms in the mud, untainted; it interacts with the world yet remains unstained.

A recurring metaphor across Buddhist scripture (the Buddha compares himself to a lotus, born in the water and rising above it unsoiled, in the Anguttara Nikaya) rather than a single traceable sutra line — the image, not a specific verse, is the point.
