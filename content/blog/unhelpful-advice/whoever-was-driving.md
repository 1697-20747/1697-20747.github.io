> Asked in 1990 who would succeed Margaret Thatcher if she were run over by a bus, Willie Whitelaw reputedly replied: "Whoever was driving the bus."

A much-repeated Westminster anecdote — the exact primary transcript has proven elusive, so treat it as folklore with a very high truth-ring rather than a verified quotation.
