> “We got the bad guy and saved the girl. But there are people in every institution and bureaucracy who can turn things to sh*t.”
>
> Harry Bosch, fictional detective, Echo Park, Chapter 30, by Michael Connelly

The point is that even when you’ve done the objectively right thing, ethically and professionally, that alone does not guarantee a good outcome for you. If your actions conflict with the personal interests, political priorities, or reputation management of someone higher up the reporting chain, you may still find yourself paying the price. Bureaucracies are often less concerned with justice than with preserving internal order, hierarchy, and appearances. Some days the 'right thing' just gets you screwed.
