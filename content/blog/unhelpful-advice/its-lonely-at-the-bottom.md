> "Never be wrong on your own"
>
> John Maynard Keynes

Except of course that he probably never said it, or if he did it was not recorded as a direct quote. What he did say was below. The motto of Bankers everywhere. We should not be churlish about this, its good advice. In theory the odds of a colossal mistake should diminish with the more eyes looking at the problem. Eventually someone who knows what they are doing should figure out the problem. The challenge here is group think, where the bias rules the day. 

So rather than being avoidance, perhaps we can restate this as its good practise to at least seek the wisdom of the crowd.

> "Worldly wisdom teaches that it is better for reputation to fail conventionally than to succeed unconventionally"
>
> The General Theory of Employment, Interest and Money (1936),
