> "It's something we've never really talked about publicly, but I actually spend more time looking at balance sheets than income statements. Wall Street often focuses on income statements, but I like to study balance sheets — ideally over an 8 to 10-year period — before I even glance at the income statement. You can't hide as much on the balance sheet. It's harder to manipulate than earnings figures." — Warren Buffett, Berkshire Hathaway Annual Shareholders Meeting, 3 May 2025.
