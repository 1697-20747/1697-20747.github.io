> "The Stone Age didn't end because we ran out of stones." — commonly attributed to Sheikh Ahmed Zaki Yamani, former Saudi oil minister.

Widely repeated, thinly sourced — energy-policy figures from Yamani to Steven Chu get credited with versions of this line. Good line regardless of who coined it first.
