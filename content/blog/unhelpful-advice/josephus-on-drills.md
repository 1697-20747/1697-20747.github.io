> "Their drills are bloodless battles, and their battles bloody drills." — Josephus, The Jewish War, 3.5.1. Their military exercises differ not at all from the real use of their arms, but every soldier is every day exercised, and that with great diligence, as if it were in time of war, which is the reason why they bear the fatigue of battles so easily.
