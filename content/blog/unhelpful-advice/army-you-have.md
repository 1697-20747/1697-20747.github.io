> "You go to war with the Army you have, not the Army you might want or wish to have at a later time." — Donald Rumsfeld, press conference, Camp Buehring, Kuwait, 8 December 2004.

It's a fool who builds his battle plan on the army he wished he had, instead of the one he's actually got.
