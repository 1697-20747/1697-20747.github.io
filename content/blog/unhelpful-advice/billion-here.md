> "A billion here, a billion there, and pretty soon you're talking real money." — popularly attributed to US Senator Everett Dirksen.

No record of him actually saying it survives in his papers or recorded speeches; quote researchers regard it as apocryphal. Still the canonical line for how casually large numbers get thrown around budgets.
