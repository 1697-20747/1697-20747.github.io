> "You don't rise to the level of your expectations, you fall to the level of your training." — popularly attributed to the ancient Greek poet Archilochus.

Near-certainly a modern saying with no ancient source; it circulates in military and Navy SEAL training culture long before anyone traced it to a 7th-century-BC poet. In a competition, you don't rise to the level of your competition — you fall to the level of your training, whoever first said so.
