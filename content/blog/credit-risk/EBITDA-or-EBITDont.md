## Overview

Below is a now classic paper on EBITDA. I would love to put actual paper here, but its not mine. It is quite easy to find though if you search on the title. It is appropriate that it is now famous, and deserves to be widely held in the public domain. A follow up by same authors would be amazing now the GFC is well past us.

Have we really learned the lessons? I suspect EBITDA is still used with purpose to overstate earnings and hide some leverage. Very much on purpose.

> Putting EBITDA In Perspective
> 
> Putting EBITDA In Perspective Ten Critical Failings Of EBITDA As The Principal Determinant Of Cash Flow
>This Special Comment is based on a speech given in New York on June 15, 2000, at the 10th Annual High Yield Bond Seminar of the New York Society of Security Analysts, by Pamela M. Stumpp, Senior Vice President, Corporate Finance Group, Moody’s Investors Service.

This remains a fine piece of critical thinking, supported by evidence. It holds as true today as it did then.

## The Paper

[Moodys EBITA Paper](https://ucema.edu.ar/u/jd/Inversiones/Articulos/Moodys_Putting_Ebitda_into_perspective.pdf)

Please read it carefully. The concept of pre capital pre tax earnings has merit. But it is hard to transfer across different industries and sectors. Unadjusted, it has the ability to overstate. if your goal is to determine pre capital (as in pre leverage) long run sustainable earnings, and it should be, you can't use EBITDA without judicious adjustments.

This paper is compulsory reading for credit and equity analysts alike.

Key quote:

> EBITDA can easily be manipulated through aggressive accounting policies relating to revenue and expense recognition, asset writedowns and concomitant adjustments to depreciation schedules, excessive adjustments in deriving “adjusted pro-forma EBITDA,” and by the timing of certain “ordinary course” asset sales.

Ponder this advice when looking at the other basket case credits laid out in their glory on this blog. What matter is the intent of the person(s) using or abusing the metric. This is unmeasurable in the truest sense, so you can only go on past evidence and likely motivation at the time. For a heavily indebted entity, the temptation will be there to continue to roll the debt, or use the financing to extract cash flows.

## Key Takeaways

- Understand your metric is the lesson. I mean that is a life lesson in general, but this instance is specific.
- Most financial statements will not give you good guidance on look forward sustaining capital expenditure. It is your job as an analyst for figure this out.
- Sustaining capex for our purpose is defined as the minium cost to sustain quality and quantity of existing production on ceteris paribus basis.
- It does not mean growth capex, that needs to be accounting for separately.
- Less about numbers being accurate, more about understand what they mean, and if they do translate that meaning to the purpose they are being used for.
