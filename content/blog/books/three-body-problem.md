Hard sci-fi on a grand scale. Starts with China's Cultural Revolution and ends at the edge of the universe. The second book, *The Dark Forest*, is the best of the three. Patience pays off.

## Observations

Great read. Skip the television series. Spend a lot of time looking up the history of Mao, and quite a bit of physics. It is amazing that these books were produced at the pace they were, given the sheer breadth of imagination contained within the pages.
