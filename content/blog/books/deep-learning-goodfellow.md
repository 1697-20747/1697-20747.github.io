THE textbook. Rigorous mathematical treatment of neural networks from first principles. Not a tutorial — a foundation. Available free at [deeplearningbook.org](https://www.deeplearningbook.org).

## Observations

A wise woman once said:

> 'Everyone thinks they are smart until they try doing Intragroup.'

For context, the Intragroup thing is a sack of spanners of complex regulation and data. This is another example, but one that is legitimately intellectually challenging.

Read the book. It is quite possible that this book is the instruction manual for the rest of our lives as AI widens. Run through the parts that are of interest. Don't copy the code — write it out, figure it out, change it, see what happens. Prepare for mental pain. This is not easy. But it is the underpinnings of AI. It will change how you think, which is the whole point.

Even if you end up using AI for code generation — and that will be all of us soon enough — this will ensure you appreciate how the plumbing works. Essential reading for any ML programmer. A life's work if you take it seriously. All available for free online. Education has been democratised. The only barrier to progress here is yourself. We should all be grateful to the authors for laying this out — it is an immensely important text.
