The foundational texts behind Beer's concept of POSIWID — the purpose of a system is what it does. A British cybernetician and management theorist, Beer argued that you cannot understand an organisation by reading its mission statement; you understand it by observing its outputs. Organisations, like all systems, do exactly what they are designed to do — even when that is the opposite of what they claim. Applies with uncomfortable precision to governments, corporations, and bureaucracies everywhere.

## Observations

Stafford's material is really interesting, even as some parts might seem dated. But the underlying thought process holds up quite well. POSIWID is now a meme as much as an observation — it often comes up in politics when the names of systems or projects run contrary to what they actually do once you prise open the lid and objectively assess function versus stated purpose.

The insight from POSIWID is the negative case: there is "no point in claiming that the purpose of a system is to do what it constantly fails to do." Delusions abound on this point.

Worth seeking out his 1970 inaugural presidential address: *'Operational Research as Revelation'*. This still rings disturbingly true several decades later. Quite a fellow — lived an interesting life, had some good thoughts on the early days of computer systems (before there really were any).

> The favoured management style therefore remains the striking of consensus. This is a prudential technique, hopefully protecting the group from lunatic intuitions which individuals may entertain. It operates at a terrible expense. The price paid is the suppression of right intuitions which lie outside the imagination, knowledge and experience of the consensus. Under this style, management becomes a Mediocrity Machine. In the fair name of "participation" it emasculates the bold and far-sighted proposal and dissipates the innovative drive.

Some illustrative examples of POSIWID in action:

- Inflation Reduction Act of 2022, US government — enacted while running a 5.5% deficit with large deficits projected as far as conceivable into the future
- UK Labour 2024 pledge for Growth, while enacting job-killing tax and minimum wage policies
- United Nations
- Any HR system ever, especially any featuring the words 'Performance Management'
- Iran's Guidance Patrols (Gasht-e Ershad)
- Russia's "special military operation" — officially aimed at the "demilitarisation and denazification" of Ukraine
