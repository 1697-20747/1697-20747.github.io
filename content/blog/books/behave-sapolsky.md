## Observations

A shocker. So almost everyone else has been focused on a game that I was unaware of, and did not care about: social status. This explains so much. There are many truths in here if you have worked at a large corporation. Or the government. The proxy of social-obsessed baboons as a model for humans is disturbing, while shockingly informative.

Don't be a psychopathic baboon. Be a chilled orangutan. Sit on your rock, eat your banana, observe with mild interest. And if one of those aggressive baboons walks over to dispense some social hierarchy on you? Beat the crap out of them — they deserve it.
