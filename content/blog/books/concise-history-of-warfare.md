Montgomery's own account of warfare from ancient times to the mid-twentieth century, written with the authority of a man who commanded armies across North Africa and Northwest Europe. Clear-eyed on strategy, leadership, and the recurring mistakes that commanders make across centuries.

## Observations

A nice summary of key battles, with a good tactical vs strategy examination. Key observation in literal sense from this book is that tactics, strategy and resources need to be in alignment to attain success. Everyone who ever worked on a large project should read this as many times as it takes to be understood. You cannot escape the constraint of resources. How many times in your life have you seen things fail because the resources — people, capital, time, technology — were materially diminished compared to the ask? Failure in such a situation is the fault of the person setting the strategy, not that of those executing. Strategy at the end of the day is the articulation of the bridge from means to the end. It reflects the painful, but necessary, choices made in the face of constraint. There are always constraints.
