### F. Why 2018 Analysis Goes Deeper Than 2015 Muddy Waters

**Muddy Waters (2015):**
- Estimated "proportional EBITDA" at €1.4B (46% of €2.6B reported)
- Based on ownership percentages in listed subsidiaries
- Used public filings from Éxito, Via Varejo, Cnova, BigC

**Our 2018 Analysis (Cash Only):**
- Tracks actual cash **remitted to parent company**
- Eliminates all unconsolidated revenues (€32.3B not remitted)
- Eliminates all affiliate revenues (€1B+ not remitted)
- Shows actual parent-level cash: €102M vs €4.3B consolidated
- **Adjustment: -88% from cash revenue; -99% from cash EBITDA**
- **Produces a more extreme (and more accurate) picture**

### H. Critical Difference: Muddy Waters vs. Our 2018 Analysis

**Muddy Waters (2015):**
- Focused on **proportional ownership model**
- Used official ownership percentages
- Assumed some dividend capacity
- Estimated EBITDA at €1.4B "proportional"

**Our 2018 Analysis:**
- Focused on **actual cash transfers**
- Used zero revenue/EBITDA for non-remitting subsidiaries

- **More conservative; more accurate; more predictive of outcomes**

**Key Difference in Approach:**
```
Muddy Waters Logic:
"Casino owns 49% of €2.6B EBITDA = €1.27B proportional EBITDA"

"Casino receives €102M cash remittance = €102M cash EBITDA

The truth turned out to be closer to our analysis: by 2024, the company had exited essentially all Latam operations, and remaining business was negligible.
