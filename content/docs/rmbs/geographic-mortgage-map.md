## Regional Insights

### London Dominance
**Why 64.5% in London?**
- Higher house prices = higher absolute loan amounts
- Better credit quality = lower default risk
- Strong HPI support = better recovery
- More stable employment = lower unemployment risk

**Implication for RMBS:**
- Provides significant stability to the pool
- Class A protection benefits from London concentration
- Economic downturn in London would impact pool significantly

### Regional Diversification
**Why other regions matter:**
- Reduces single-market risk
- Provides geographic hedging
- Captures regional economic variations
- Spreads unemployment risk

**Implication for RMBS:**
- If London struggles, other regions provide stability
- Regional economic variations create natural hedging
- Different regional cycles reduce correlation
