Still the most ambitious thing anyone has ever written. Read it with a good commentary — Dorothy Sayers' translation and notes are an excellent entry point.

## Observations

An old book, with some great lines and observational psychology. Even without the science, they could intuit so much. Worth the read, many times, even if you are not pursuing Christian roots. My favourite lines are the usual "Abandon all hope, ye who enter here" (*Lasciate ogne speranza, voi ch'intrate*); and most of all Canto IX towards the end. The first step to progress really does require self-reflection in an honest mirror, whereupon you may not progress until you see your flaws for what they are. How many of us have the humility for this?

> Thither did we approach; and the first stair  
> Was marble white, so polished and so smooth,  
> I mirrored myself therein as I appear.  
>  
> The second, tinct of deeper hue than perse,  
> Was of a calcined and uneven stone,  
> Cracked all asunder lengthwise and across.  
>  
> The third, that uppermost rests massively,  
> Porphyry seemed to me, as flaming red  
> As blood that from a vein is spirting forth.
