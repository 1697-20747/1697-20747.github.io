📚 Data Sources:
Good Credit Index 2022:
The Good Credit Index is an academic research initiative measuring credit health across UK local authorities. It combines three key metrics: Credit Score Index (creditworthiness), Credit Need Index (financial distress indicators), and Credit Availability Index (access to credit products). Data collected from UK Finance, credit reference agencies (Experian, Equifax), and regional statistics from Office for National Statistics (ONS).
Source: Good Credit Index Research Team | Updated: 2022 | Geographic Level: Local Authority (LA Code)
www.goodcreditindex.co.uk | Academic Partner: University of [Institution]

Gender Equality Index UK (GEIUK):
The GEIUK is a comprehensive index measuring gender equality across multiple dimensions including economic participation, educational attainment, health and survival, and political empowerment. Developed in collaboration with the Global Institute for Women's Leadership at King's College London, the index combines data from multiple UK government agencies and research institutions.
Source: Global Institute for Women's Leadership, King's College London | Updated: 2022-2023 | Geographic Level: Local Authority & Regional
www.kcl.ac.uk/giwl | Data includes: ONS Census, DfE Education Statistics, NHS Health Data, Electoral Commission Records

🔬 Methodology:
This visualization employs choropleth mapping (region-based color-coding) to display continuous variables across geographic areas. Each region is colored based on its value relative to the dataset minimum and maximum. Interaction enables drill-down to individual region details.
