Key Policy Regimes
1970–79 — Expansionary fiscal/monetary policy; OPEC oil shocks amplify inflation to 24.2% peak (1975)
1979–92 — Thatcher monetarism; M3 targets; inflation squeezed from 18% to sub-4% by 1983
1992–2007 — BoE independence (1997); inflation targeting; M4 growth elevated but CPI anchored
2008–20 — QE expands base money; M4 growth muted; CPI decoupled from monetary base
2020–23 — COVID M4 surge (+14.2% 2020); supply shocks; CPI peaks 9.1% (2022)
