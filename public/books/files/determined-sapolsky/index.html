Observations#

This is a very hard read. I don’t mean technically — I mean emotionally. The notes are the technical read. And yes, don’t skip them; read them all. It is a mini degree in neuroscience for the price of a book.

I am still very torn on my position on this book. As of February 2026, the news broke of the conviction of a killer with profound mental health challenges — Deividas Skebas, 26, who stabbed Lilia Valutyte to death in a random attack outside the shop where her mother worked in Boston, Lincolnshire, on 28 July 2022. A jury at Lincoln Crown Court convicted him following an eight-day trial. There are multiple tragedies at play here: the young girl, the absence of mental health residential facilities, and a couple more I can think of. What a senseless waste.

Read the book. Despair.
