Determined

Sapolsky’s follow-up makes the full case against free will. Provocative, rigorous, and uncomfortable. Read Behave first. Observations This is a very hard read. I don’t mean technically — I mean emotionally. The notes are the technical read. And yes, don’t skip them; read them all. It is a mini degree in neuroscience for the price of a book. I am still very torn on my position on this book. The Divine Comedy

Still the most ambitious thing anyone has ever written. Read it with a good commentary — Dorothy Sayers’ translation and notes are an excellent entry point. Observations An old book, with some great lines and observational psychology. Even without the science, they could intuit so much. Worth the read, many times, even if you are not pursuing Christian roots. My favourite lines are the usual “Abandon all hope, ye who enter here” (Lasciate ogne speranza, voi ch’intrate); and most of all Canto IX towards the end. The first step to progress really does require self-reflection in an honest mirror, whereupon you may not progress until you see your flaws for what they are. How many of us have the humility for this? ...
